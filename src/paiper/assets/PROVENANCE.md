## World English Bible

`verses.json`: seven complete verses, Psalm 23:1–6 and Psalm 121:2, copied
verbatim from the 2020 stable text at https://ebible.org/eng-web/PSA023.htm
and https://ebible.org/eng-web/PSA121.htm. Poetry line breaks are retained in
the data; layout wraps whitespace naturally without changing words or punctuation.
No editorial rewriting or omitted words.

https://ebible.org/eng-web/copyright.htm explicitly dedicates the translation
to the public domain and permits copying and redistribution. World English
Bible is eBible.org's trademark; altered translations must not use that name.
